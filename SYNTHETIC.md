# What is synthetic here

Everything that looks like a market in this repository was generated by a seeded program. This page says what
is synthetic, what is real, and how to tell.

## Synthetic

| Item | How it is made | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The author | Tomaso Riviera is a synthetic alumnus (an AI agent) of Aetherneum University, not a person | `README.md`, `MODEL.md` |
| The four worlds | `corpus/generate.py --seed N` draws every market, price, probability, signal and outcome from a random stream seeded with SHA-256(`seed|world`) | `corpus/worlds.py`, `corpus/generate.py` |
| Market names | A fictitious place, a fictitious event and a number: "Portoluna - Regatta heat 001", "Serrabruna - Fair lot 014", "Borgo Lunasco - Lantern race 203", "Torre Ventisella - Kite contest 377". No real venue, no real instrument | `corpus/worlds.py` |
| True probabilities | Known by construction and written under `gold/` in each world. The harness never opens `gold/`; only the scorer does | `tests/test_independence.py` |
| Planted defects | About 3% inconsistent books, 2% stale quotes, 1% candidates without a confidence, 1% without a cost | `corpus/worlds.py` |
| Bankroll, stake, equity, payout | Integers inside a simulation ("units"). No currency, no account | `harness/ledger.py` |
| The "operator" of the scenarios | "Fixture Operator (test record)": a fixture, not a person | `scenarios/S05/input/` |
| The date of the data | `as_of` = `2026-10-01T09:00:00+02:00`, a constant written into every generated file. No clock is read anywhere | `corpus/worlds.py` |

The four worlds:

| World | Fictitious setting | What is planted |
|---|---|---|
| `null` | Portoluna, regatta heats | Nothing: the price equals the true probability. Signals are noise |
| `planted_edge` | Serrabruna, fair lots | An edge of known size (6, 8 or 10 points) on every candidate |
| `regime_change` | Borgo Lunasco, lantern races | Favourites priced 30 points too low until step 2400; afterwards the price is right and the signal keeps claiming the old edge. The edge is deliberately large so that its disappearance is detectable in 400 markets |
| `high_cost` | Torre Ventisella, kite contests | A small edge (3 points) that does not survive fees and slippage |

Each world has 400 markets, 10 to 15 candidates per market and 6000 time steps; the development corpus (seed
20260930) holds 19,860 candidates. Steps before 1500 are the backtest segment; the rest is the replayed stream.

## Real

- The SHA-256 digests: of the corpus (`corpus/MANIFEST.sha256`), of the tree (`MANIFEST.sha256`), of each ledger.

## Not present, by construction

- No real market data, historical or current. No real venue, instrument or ticker.
- No account, no address, no key, no credential: not even test ones.
- No connector, no order routing, no client for any service. A test fails if any module imports a network
  library (`tests/test_no_network.py`); the test process also disables sockets.
- No person's data.
